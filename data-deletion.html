Data Deletion

Order Scout for Threads · Last updated: 2026-10-21

You can have all data that Order Scout holds about your Threads account deleted in either of two ways.

1. Remove the app in Threads

  1. Open Threads → Settings → Account → Website permissions.
  2. Find Order Scout and remove it.

Meta notifies us automatically, and we delete your access token and every record linked to your account.

2. Ask us directly

Send your Threads username to @Arystanbekleos on Telegram or to +7 708 008 7580 on WhatsApp with the words “delete my data”. We delete the data within 7 days and confirm when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your Threads user ID, username and access token.
  • Search history and any stored posts linked to your account.

Public posts by other people that matched a search are deleted automatically after 30 days, as described in the Privacy Policy.
